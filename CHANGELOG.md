## 1.0.0

### 🚀 New Features

- Play your local music library in Lumide: scan a folder, then play, pause, skip, shuffle, and repeat.
- Status bar controls, with the current lyric line or track title next to them.
- Synced lyrics from `.lrc` files next to your tracks, in the status bar and the player panel.
- Player panel with scrolling lyrics, a seek bar, and playback buttons.
- Playlist picker and a controls menu from the status bar.
- Keyboard shortcuts for play/pause, next, and previous.
- Audio engine (libmpv) set up automatically on macOS, using the system libmpv on Linux, or a custom one via `muse.libmpvPath`.
